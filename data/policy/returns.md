# Returns policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Day 30 counts. On day 31 and after, we cannot accept a return or give a refund or store credit.

## What cannot be returned
Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged. Clearance items are marked final sale on the order.

## Refund method
Refunds go to the original payment method or to store credit. The customer chooses. Store credit is available right away.

## Refund timing
Refunds to the original payment method take 5 to 7 business days after the return is processed.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free. The customer gets a prepaid label by email after the return is processed.

## Partial returns
A customer can return some items from an order and keep the rest. Each item is checked on its own.
