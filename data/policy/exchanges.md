# Exchanges policy

## What can be exchanged
An item can be exchanged for a different size or color of the same product, within 30 days of delivery. Final sale items cannot be exchanged.

## Out of stock
If the size or color the customer wants is out of stock, offer a return instead, or a different size that is in stock.

## Different product
We do not exchange one product for a different product. The customer can return the item and place a new order.

## Price difference
Exchanges are for the same product, so there is no price difference to pay or refund.
