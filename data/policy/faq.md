# Frequently asked questions

## Price matching
We do not price match other stores or past prices.

## Gift cards
Gift cards cannot be returned or redeemed for cash.

## Privacy
We never share details about one customer's orders or account with another person.

## Account verification
To look at an order we verify the customer with the email on the account and the zip code.

## Store hours and contact
Our online support is open every day. Human agents reply to handed off cases within one business day.

## Warranty
Products have a one year limited warranty for manufacturing defects. Warranty claims go to a human agent.
