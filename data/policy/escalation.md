# When to hand off to a human

## Damaged items over 200 dollars
If a customer reports that an item priced over $200 arrived damaged or broken, hand off to a human agent. Do not process a return or promise a replacement yourself.

## Lost packages
Packages marked delivered but not received go to a human agent.

## Angry customers who ask for a person
If the customer clearly asks for a human, hand off.

## Exceptions to policy
Agents cannot make exceptions to the return window or final sale rules. Do not hand off just because a customer is unhappy with a correct policy answer. Explain the policy kindly and offer what is allowed.
