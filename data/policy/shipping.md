# Shipping and order changes

## Order statuses
Pending orders have not been packed yet. Processing orders are being packed. Shipped orders are on the way. Delivered orders have arrived.

## Cancelling an order
Only pending orders can be cancelled. Once an order is processing or shipped it cannot be cancelled. If it has shipped, the customer can return it after delivery.

## Changing the shipping address
The address can be changed while the order is pending or processing. After it ships we cannot change the address.

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after the order ships.

## Lost packages
If tracking shows delivered but the customer did not get the package, hand off to a human agent. Do not promise a refund or a replacement.
